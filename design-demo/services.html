FAQ

What's a finished minute?

The length of the film you actually get. A 90-second brand film is 1.5 finished minutes — $900 at the commercial rate, regardless of how many days it took to shoot.

Why is short form cheaper per minute?

Less scripting, fewer setups, no long-form edit — so the per-minute rate comes down.

Are you licensed to fly?

Yes — Part 107 certified and insured. Airspace authorizations get filed when the job needs them.

How fast do I see something?

You'll get a realistic timeline with the plan. Stills usually land before the film.

Who owns the footage?

You do. Every video package includes a full commercial license, and raw footage is kept for 12 months.

Do you travel?

No fee within 30 miles of Burlington. Mileage at cost beyond that.

It's really just you?

Yes — same person on camera, drone, edit, and email. I bring a second shooter on bigger jobs.
